About the Workshop

Precision at Every Scale

NexGen Build is a premier industrial solutions provider based in Dammam. We specialize in high-precision CNC Laser Cutting, Engraving, and Advanced Routing services, catering to the evolving needs of the construction, oil and gas, and architectural sectors in Saudi Arabia.

With a commitment to technical excellence and operational reliability, we bridge the gap between complex engineering designs and high-quality physical components. Our facility is equipped with state-of-the-art Fiber Laser technology, ensuring every project is executed with micron-level accuracy.
